Metric definitions

MetricDefinitionCalculation
Licensed usersPeople who held a Claude licence on at least one day of the month.Distinct users with a licence in the month
Active dayA day on which a person had at least one interaction with Claude.Any scored activity on that day
Active usersA monthly measure: people with 5 or more active days in the month. There is no weekly or daily active-user figure.Distinct users with active days ≥ 5
Adoption rateThe share of licensed users who were active in the month. No target is set.Active users ÷ licensed users
Engagement scoreWeighted points for what a person did with Claude, using the scoring rules below. Higher weights go to deeper use.Sum of engagement points
Engagement benchmarkA typical day's score for each role: the median daily score of active user-days in the previous three complete months, recalculated each quarter. Q1 2026 uses its own months because there is no earlier data.Daily benchmark; weekly = daily × 5; monthly = daily × 20
% of benchmarkHow a person's month compares with a typical month for their role.Monthly score ÷ role's monthly benchmark
Meeting benchmarkThe share of active users whose monthly score reached their role's monthly benchmark.Active users at 100% or more ÷ active users
User segmentIdle: no activity in the month. Light: under 50% of benchmark. Regular: 50% to 150%. Power: over 150%.Based on % of benchmark for the month
Licence costThe fixed fee for each licence, spread evenly across the days it was held.USD 20 per user per month (assumed)
Usage costThe cost of tokens consumed, billed on top of the licence.Sum of usage cost
Total costLicence cost plus usage cost.Licence + usage
Cost per active userWhat the month cost for each active user. It includes the licences of people who were not active.Total cost ÷ active users
Idle licenceA licence held at the end of the range by someone with no activity in the last two months of the range.No active day in the last two months
Days idleDays since the person's last active day, or since the licence was assigned if they never used it.Range end − last active day
User decisionAn accept or reject of a Claude Code edit made by a person at a prompt. Edits approved automatically by settings or hooks are excluded.Decision source is a user choice
Acceptance rateThe share of user decisions that accepted Claude's suggested edit.Accepted ÷ (accepted + rejected)
Auto-approved shareThe share of all Claude Code edits approved without a person reviewing them.Automatic decisions ÷ all decisions

Engagement scoring rules

Each activity is turned into units, multiplied by its weight, and capped per person per day. Caps stop one high-volume activity, such as chat messages, from dominating the score.

ActivityCategoryWeightCounted asDaily cap (points)
Chat messagesBasic prompt11 unit per 2 messages10
Files uploadedSummarise / extract2Each fileNone
Web searchesSummarise / extract2Each search10
Artifacts createdGenerate3Each artifactNone
Office add-in messagesGenerate31 unit per 2 messages9
Projects usedAdvanced / agentic4Each distinct projectNone
Connector callsAdvanced / agentic4Each call12
Claude Code sessionsAdvanced / agentic4Each sessionNone
Cowork sessionsAdvanced / agentic4Each sessionNone

Example: 12 chat messages and one Claude Code session score 6 × 1 + 1 × 4 = 10 points.

Engagement benchmark by role

Daily benchmark in points. Each quarter uses the previous quarter's data; Q1 has no earlier data, so it uses its own. Weekly is daily × 5 and monthly is daily × 20. Q4 applies from the October refresh.

VisualRequirementWhat it shows
Headline figuresFR-1Six figures for the last month of the selected range, each compared with the month before. A green arrow is an improvement and a red arrow a decline. For the two cost figures, red means spend rose and green means it fell.

Idle licencesFR-5Everyone holding a licence who has not used Claude in the last two months of the range, longest idle first. These are the first candidates to reassign or drop.
